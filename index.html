1. About this policy

This policy explains how SiPDF handles information. It describes the version of the application named above.

Statements about advertising, analytics and data collection describe this version of the app. Section 9 explains how any future change is communicated.

In this policy, "we" means the publisher of SiPDF and "you" means the person using the app.

2. Summary

SiPDF is an offline PDF reader and toolkit.

  • No account and no sign-in.
  • Your documents are opened and processed on your device.
  • This version collects no personal data and no usage data, and transmits nothing to us or to any third party.
  • This version contains no advertising, no analytics, and no tracking.
  • A document leaves the app only when you choose to share it.

3. Information we collect

3.1 Data sent to us or to third parties

None.

SiPDF has no servers, no user accounts, and no backend service. The application does not transmit any information off your device.

This version of the app does not request internet access, so it cannot send data over a network. You can confirm this yourself: the app's permission list is visible under Settings → Apps → SiPDF → Permissions on your device, and in the app's Google Play listing.

3.2 Data stored locally on your device

SiPDF saves the following in its own private application storage. This information stays on your device and is accessible only to SiPDF:

What is storedPurpose
Your app settings (theme, resume-last-page, keep-screen-awake)To keep the app working the way you configured it
List of recently opened documentsSo you can reopen what you were reading
Documents you marked as favouritesSo you can return to them quickly
Your reading position in each documentSo a document reopens at the page you left
References to documents you have openedSo those documents can be reopened without asking you to select them again
A temporary working copy of an open documentRequired to display and modify the file

You can remove this information at any time:

  • Settings → Clear recent history clears the recent list and keeps favourites.
  • Settings → Reset SiPDF clears settings, favourites, recent list and reading positions.
  • Uninstalling the app removes all of it.

Clearing or resetting does not delete your PDF files. Those are your files, stored where you keep them, and SiPDF does not remove them.

3.3 Information we do not collect

We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, location, advertising identifier, device identifier, IP address, list of installed apps, usage analytics, session recordings, or the contents of your documents.

4. Permissions

This version of SiPDF requests no Android permissions. In particular it does not request:

  • Internet access
  • Storage access (including all-files access)
  • Media, photo, or file library access
  • Location, camera, microphone, contacts, phone, or SMS

To open a document, SiPDF uses Android's own document picker. Android displays the picker, you choose a file or folder, and the app receives access only to what you selected. SiPDF cannot browse or scan the rest of your storage.

5. How your documents are handled

  • PDFs are opened, displayed and modified entirely on your device.
  • Every tool in the app — merge, split, extract pages, delete pages, reorder, rotate, images-to-PDF, compress, and document information — runs locally on your device. No document is uploaded to any server for processing, at any time, for any feature.
  • To display or modify a document, SiPDF creates a temporary working copy in its private application storage. That copy stays on your device and is removed when Android clears the app's temporary storage or when you uninstall the app.
  • If you open a password-protected PDF, the password you enter is used only to unlock that document for viewing. It is not saved, not recorded, and not transmitted.

6. Sharing with other applications

A document leaves SiPDF only when you explicitly choose to share it or open it in another app. Android's share menu then passes the file to the application you select.

That application is not covered by this policy. Once a document is handed to another app — email, messaging, cloud storage, or another PDF reader — it is governed by that application's own privacy policy and terms. We have no control over, and no visibility into, what that application does with your file.

7. Third-party components

SiPDF includes third-party open-source components for functions such as displaying PDFs and selecting files. In this version, none of these components is an advertising, analytics, attribution, tracking, or crash-reporting service, and none of them transmits your information.

A complete list of these components and their licences is available inside the app under Settings → About SiPDF → Open-source licenses.

8. Data retention and security

Because no information is collected or transmitted, there is no data held on any server, no copy of your information in our possession, and nothing for us to disclose, sell, or expose in a data breach.

On your device, the information described in section 3.2 is kept until you clear it or uninstall the app. It is stored in the application's private storage area, which Android keeps isolated from other applications. Automatic device backup of this data is disabled, so it is not copied off your device by Android's backup service.

We do not sell personal information, and we do not share it with third parties for advertising or for any other purpose.

9. Children

SiPDF is a general-purpose document reader. It is not directed at children. It does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children, because it does not collect information at all.

10. Changes to this policy

SiPDF will receive updates over time, and a future version may handle information differently. Features that would change this policy include analytics, crash reporting, advertising, or an optional account or cloud sync.

If any future version of SiPDF introduces any of these, then in the same release that introduces it:

  1. This policy is updated with a new effective date and an entry in section 12.
  2. The privacy information shown inside the app is updated to match.
  3. The Data Safety section of the app's Google Play listing is updated to match.

We will not introduce data collection without disclosing it, and we will not declare data collection that is not taking place. The statements in section 2 are scoped to a specific version precisely so that a later version can differ without any part of this document having been inaccurate.

Continued use of the app after an updated policy takes effect constitutes acceptance of the revised policy. If you do not accept a change, you may stop using the app and uninstall it, which removes the locally stored information described in section 3.2.

11. Your rights

Depending on where you live, data protection laws such as the GDPR (European Economic Area and United Kingdom) and the CCPA (California) may give you rights over personal information held about you, including rights of access, correction, deletion, portability, and objection.

In this version of SiPDF, we hold no personal information about you, so there is nothing for us to provide, correct, export, or delete on your behalf. The information described in section 3.2 is under your direct control on your own device and can be removed at any time using the app's own settings, as described in that section.
